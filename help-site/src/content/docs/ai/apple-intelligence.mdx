## What you will do

Find the on-device provider and understand its availability requirements.

## Before you start

- An active Standard or Pro subscription, or an eligible trial.
- An eligible physical device on iOS/iPadOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model ready.

[Download the tutorial workspace](/vibeit/help/downloads/vibeit-tutorials.zip)

## Check the result

Once available, activate the provider and try a short request. Simulator availability is not evidence of whether your physical device supports it.

## If it does not work

- If the provider is configured but not used, select Use This Provider and check the active badge.
- For sign-in or key errors, repair the provider configuration before retrying a short request.
- Provider choices can depend on your App Store region, subscription and the provider account.
